01. About

I'm a software developer based in Brussels, building products end to end — from backend services to the interfaces people actually touch. My background blends academic computer science with hands‑on, ship‑it‑fast training from BeCode's Junior Web Developer bootcamp (AI & DevSecOps track), on top of studies at Vrije Universiteit Brussel.

Right now most of my energy goes into Inkbind, an all‑in‑one PDF toolkit I'm building across web, desktop and mobile. Outside of that I take on freelance builds — business sites, invoicing tools, WordPress plugins — and I still enjoy the occasional detour into compilers and reinforcement learning, just to keep the fundamentals sharp.
